Who it is for

Anyone serious about building something

A business, a nonprofit, a community project, a side venture, or a research idea that should reach real people. Founders, small business owners, Queens College and CUNY students, faculty, staff, and alumni. There is no stage requirement. Someone who has not earned a dollar yet and someone ten years into a business work the same method, each on their own venture.

Track one

Build

You are at the idea or early-customer stage. You work through the whole venture, one domain a week: customers, offer, marketing, sales, operations, finance, people. By week eight you know which parts hold up.

Track two

Grow

You already run a venture or an organization. You pick the one constraint holding it back and work on it for eight weeks with the group behind you, testing a change every single week.

How a session runs

Ninety minutes, the same structure every week

The structure does not change, so it becomes a habit instead of a surprise. More than half the room's time goes to work on your own venture, not to a lecture.

15 check-in
20 principle
50 build

  • 15 min Check-inWhat happened with last week's experiment. Numbers, observations, or a real person's words. Not impressions.
  • 20 min PrincipleOne useful business concept, taught through a participant's real situation that week.
  • 50 min BuildGuided work on your venture with peers, a facilitator, and AI as a thinking partner. Ends with a peer challenge on what you just made.
  • 5 min CommitOne experiment for the week. Low cost, done before the next session, and it produces a number or a real person's words.
The method

Six questions, pointed at your venture

This is the scientific method applied to a business. You run the same six questions every week, and the answers move from guesses to evidence as the weeks go on.

01EvidenceWhat do we actually know?
02GoalWhat are we trying to accomplish?
03ConstraintWhat is getting in the way?
04GapWhat do we still need to learn or build?
05ExperimentWhat is the smallest useful test?
06CommitmentWhat will you do, and by when?
The eight weeks

One module a week, applied the same day

You may miss one of the eight. Plan for about two hours between sessions: a customer conversation, a test, a fix. It is fieldwork, not homework.
